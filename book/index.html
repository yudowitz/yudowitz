Book description — in the author’s words

My life is in danger. My baby was murdered. Someone who saved my life is said to have been killed. I am demanding FBI action.

I am Larry Margulies, also known as Lawrence Yudowitz.

For years, I have documented death threats, assassination attempts, and events spanning multiple countries. My criminal complaints raise questions about the people involved, the actions taken, and the government records that could help establish what happened.

I am also searching for the Good Samaritan who saved my life and was later said to have been killed. I want to establish who this person was and what happened.

My public case files include complaints and supplements submitted to the NBI Philippines, Aventura Police Department, and U.S. Department of State Office of Inspector General. They also raise questions concerning the Yudowitz family's State Department liaison and a warning from the Boston Passport Agency.

I am releasing these public case files and inviting the public to examine them because Aventura Police Department has stated this is a matter for the FBI, the State Department has declined to assist, one of the people threatening me claimed to have people inside the FBI, and when I contacted the FBI I was told not to call back.

About Larry Margulies

Larry Margulies, also known as Lawrence Yudowitz, is a Boston native and the son of Dr. Bernard Yudowitz. He is the author of Targeted: Son of Dr. Bernard Yudowitz — A Boston Native’s Call for FBI Action and the complainant-affiant in the public filings presented in the book.

About the earlier books

Before Targeted, I published more than fifty ebooks under different pen names, covers, and titles. I wrote many of them on a phone, often while traveling, as new events occurred. They contain errors and unedited passages and provide contemporaneous background and additional discussion.

I recommend readers start with Targeted: Son of Dr. Bernard Yudowitz, the current compilation of public complaints, supplements, and supporting records. Consult the original source documents for their exact wording and pagination.
